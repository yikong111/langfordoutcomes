Free checklist — six HARD gates

Mark YES only if true today with evidence you can show. Missing evidence = treat as NO. Any HARD NO → SUBMIT_BLOCKED.

HARD verdict

  • All 1–5 = YES and (6 = YES or OWNER_GATE with Owner ready) → may prepare proposal
  • Any HARD = NO → SUBMIT_BLOCKED — do not send
  • OWNER_GATE and Owner not available → park in queue — do not bypass

Decision: ☐ PASS · ☐ BLOCKED · ☐ OWNER_GATE

SOFT signals (optional) — never alone kill or approve
  • AI / Bot advantage on this job?
  • Why not DIY (buyer side)?
  • Competitor density · Repeat potential · Copyability
  • Owner attention tax if won

EV ≈ P(win) × expected gross margin − proposal cost − Owner cost.

FAQ

Is the checklist free?
Yes — the full hard-gate method and the in-browser scorer are free on this page.
How does the scorer work?
Client-side JavaScript applies a transparent keyword/heuristic rubric to the six HARD gates. It is not a large language model and does not call a paid API. Treat FAIL as SKIP; treat CAUTION as “verify with evidence.”
Do I need Upwork?
No. Same gates work on any paid bid/quote surface. Examples use Upwork language because that is where search intent is loudest.
Will this make me money?
No tool guarantees revenue. This only helps you not submit jobs that fail basic cash gates. We do not claim past sales or client wins.
Is this another AI proposal spammer?
No. It is a stop/go gate. Soft notes never alone approve a bid.
Can I share this page?
Yes. Linking the free checklist into helpful answers is encouraged; hard-sell spam is not.
